# Reference shows — what each teaches Next Frontier

Researched 2026-10-20. Full notes (Thai): `NextFrontier/note.md`.

| Show | Format | Lesson for Next Frontier | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ลงทุนแมน (TH) | Solo narrator business stories | Shocking result → twist ("แต่ที่เด็ดกว่านั้นคือ…") → name the topic → 1–2 questions → signature line "ลงทุนแมนจะเล่าให้ฟัง" → background in time order → spoken section markers ("กลุ่มแรก คือ…") → numbers tied to tangible things / multiples → one lesson + metaphor ("ขายพลั่วในยุคตื่นทอง") → hedged close. Short sentences, friend-telling-a-story tone. Pick relatable topics; accuracy over frequency; keep a consistent identity. | [longtunman.com/66956](https://www.longtunman.com/66956), [The Secret Sauce EP74](https://thestandard.co/podcast/thesecretsauce74/) |
| The Indicator (NPR Planet Money) | ≤ 10 min, daily | One story or one big idea per episode. | [NPR](https://www.npr.org/about-npr/567830557/npr-launches-the-indicator-from-planet-money) |
| The Daily (NYT) | One story per episode | Pre-writing questions: what is the story, what are the stakes, why it matters, what happened, who the players are, what the takeaways are. | [radioinfo](https://radioinfo.com.au/?p=235276) |
| Acquired | Long-form company histories | The news event (deal, IPO, funding) is only the door into the story; choose the starting point carefully so the event has context. | [Princeton Alumni Weekly](https://paw.princeton.edu/article/podcast-spotlight-david-rosenthal-07-takes-closer-look-ipos-and-acquisitions), [Deal Lift](https://dealift.beehiiv.com/p/from-an-idea-in-a-bar-to-a-media-powerhouse) |
| Modern MBA / The Business Caselette | YouTube business case studies | Each episode needs its own insight; wrap data, context and metrics in one story; a clear agenda up front for 5–10 min videos. | [Substack](https://internetstuff.substack.com/p/a-studied-contention), [Poets&Quants](https://poetsandquants.com/2021/08/11/the-disruptors-the-business-caselette/) |
| How I Built This | Founder interviews | For future interview episodes: emotional arc through doubt and failure before success; leave room for silence. | [Podcastle](https://podcastle.ai/blog/how-i-built-this-podcast) |
| The Secret Sauce / Mission To The Moon (TH) | Executive-perspective business podcasts | Turn stories into lessons Thai professionals can apply. | [Krungsri](https://www.krungsri.com/th/plearn-plearn/interest-podcast) |
| Writing for the ear | Radio/audio writing guides | One idea per sentence; short sentences; round numbers and give context; read aloud. | [UBC](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:LFS400/Workshops/Writing_For_the_Ear), [MX Group](https://www.themxgroup.com/resources/how-to-write-for-human-ear/), [Media Helping Media](https://mediahelpingmedia.org/how-to/how-to-write-a-radio-news-script) |
| YouTube script playbooks | Creator guides (vendor sites — treat as rough guidance) | 0–5 s attention, 5–15 s promise, 15–30 s stakes; no "welcome back" first; open loops; change pace every 60–90 s; CTA follows from the content. | [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-write-a-youtube-script), [Hooked](https://www.hooked.so/blog/script-for-youtube-video), [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/structure-youtube-videos-watch-time-seo) |
